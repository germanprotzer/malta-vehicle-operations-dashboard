# malta-vehicle-operations-dashboard

Excel portfolio project analyzing public vehicle data in Malta to identify market structure, energy trends, and maintenance-related business opportunities.

---

Most vehicle market data can be difficult to interpret when it is spread across tables, categories, and registration types.

This dashboard transforms public vehicle data from Malta into a clear, business-oriented view to support market monitoring, operational planning, and service agreement opportunity analysis.

---

## What you can see

• Total licensed vehicles in Malta
• Main vehicle categories by stock size
• Vehicle stock distribution by energy type
• Top newly licensed vehicle categories
• Key insights related to maintenance products, service agreements, and operational reporting

---

## Dashboard sections

**Market Overview**
High-level KPIs showing total licensed vehicles, petrol vehicles, diesel vehicles, and EV + hybrid share.

**Vehicle Category Analysis**
Top licensed vehicle categories in Malta, highlighting the largest segments of the market.

**Energy Type Analysis**
Distribution of licensed vehicles by petrol, diesel, electric, and plug-in hybrid categories.

**New Registration Activity**
Top newly licensed vehicle categories during Q4 2025.

---

## Key insights

• Passenger cars dominate Malta’s licensed vehicle stock and new registrations.
• Petrol and diesel vehicles still represent the majority of the market.
• Electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles represent a smaller share, but are relevant for future operational tracking.
• The data suggests potential opportunities for maintenance products, service agreements, market monitoring, and operational reporting.

---

## Business relevance

This dashboard provides a clear overview of Malta’s licensed vehicle market, highlighting the main vehicle categories, new registration activity, and energy-type distribution.

The analysis can support business decisions related to product positioning, service agreement opportunities, maintenance demand, operational planning, and market monitoring.
